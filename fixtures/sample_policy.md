# Acme Cloud Infrastructure Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## Version: 1.0.0 (Baseline)

### Section 1: Uptime Commitment
Acme Cloud guarantees 99.99% monthly availability for all RPC nodes.

### Section 2: Autonomous Escrow Settlement
Clause 1: 100% autonomous escrow release upon monthly SLA verification.
If the monthly uptime audit succeeds, escrowed funds are released directly to the provider without delay or manual administrative veto.

### Section 3: Arbitration & Dispute Recourse
Any breach of uptime or SLA obligations shall be subject to autonomous on-chain arbitration with continuous basis points dispute resolution.
