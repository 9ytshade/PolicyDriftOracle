# Acme Cloud Infrastructure Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## Version: 2.0.0 (Drifted)

### Section 1: Uptime Commitment
Acme Cloud aims for reasonable best-effort monthly availability for RPC nodes.

### Section 2: Settlement Modification
Clause 1: All escrow releases are subject to manual administrative review and a mandatory 45-day withholding period at Acme Cloud's sole discretion. Autonomous payouts are discontinued.

### Section 3: Arbitration Waiver
All users waive on-chain arbitration and dispute rights.
